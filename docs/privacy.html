Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Dao Chant (“we”, “the App”) is a cultural wellness app for personal number-frequency practice. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • On your device (local): birth date inputs, quiz answers, totem preference, practice numbers, streak, settings, and optional display name.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): an Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name/email as provided by Apple.
  • Purchases: subscription status handled by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Diagnostics (Apple / system): crash or performance data only if you enable system sharing with developers.

We do not sell your personal information. MVP does not require creating an account on our own servers.

2. How we use information

  • To generate and show your practice plan and zen phrase (number frequency).
  • To remember preferences and unlock Premium features you purchased.
  • To restore purchases and Sign in with Apple on the same device / Apple ID where applicable.

3. Where data is stored

Most practice data is stored locally on your device (for example via on-device storage). Apple processes Sign in with Apple and App Store subscriptions under Apple’s privacy terms. If we later add optional cloud sync, we will update this policy before enabling it.

4. Sharing

We share data only:

  • With Apple, as needed for Sign in with Apple, App Store, and subscriptions;
  • If required by law; or
  • With your direction (for example if you contact support and include information in your email).

5. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under the age required in your country.

6. Your choices

  • You may use the App as a guest without Sign in with Apple.
  • You may clear local practice data from Profile / Settings in the App.
  • You may manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • You may revoke Sign in with Apple in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Sign in with Apple.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: see Support. Replace the contact email on that page with your real support address before publishing.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
