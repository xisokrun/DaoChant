Dao Sleep

  • Watch tab → Connect Health. Allow Sleep Analysis when iOS asks.
  • There is no separate Watch app. Watch sleep syncs into Apple Health; the iPhone app reads it.
  • If you deny Health access, the rest of the app still works.
  • Dao Sleep is a paid download for core features (no subscription maze).

Dao Chant (legacy)

To cancel or change a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dao Chant. Refunds are handled by Apple under App Store policies (reportaproblem.apple.com).
